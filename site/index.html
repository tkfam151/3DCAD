Meet Pip, your helper

Pip lives in the corner of the app. Click Pip and ask things like “how do I make a hole?”, “what's wrong?” or “how big is it?”. You get an answer and a button that does the job for you.

Pip cheers when a step works, warns you about errors and naps when you're away. Press H to hide or bring back Pip. It works offline, with no account needed.

Help, the app won't open

Mac: the icon bounces in the Dock, or macOS says the app is blocked

The app isn't notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer account, so newer macOS versions block it. First try System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. If there's no button, open Terminal and paste this line:

pkill -9 -f "OpenCAD Studio"; mkdir -p ~/Applications; rm -rf ~/Applications/"OpenCAD Studio.app"; ditto --noextattr --noqtn "/Applications/OpenCAD Studio.app" ~/Applications/"OpenCAD Studio.app" && codesign --force --deep --sign - ~/Applications/"OpenCAD Studio.app" && open ~/Applications/"OpenCAD Studio.app"

It makes a copy in your personal Applications folder, signs it on your Mac and opens it. If Terminal says Operation not permitted, turn on Terminal under System Settings → Privacy & Security → App Management and try again. You can also skip all of this and use the browser version.

Windows: “Windows protected your PC”

Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't code-signed yet.

Can it open Fusion, STEP or IGES files?

Not yet. Export an STL or OBJ from the other program and open that. OpenCAD opens and edits those.
